# Social media

How Sprecher East shows up on Nextdoor, Facebook and other platforms. It is an unofficial site run
by one resident, with AI tools doing research and drafting, and every post says so. Tone and word
choices are in the [brand voice guidelines](../brand-voice-guidelines.md); sourcing is in
[content marketing](content-marketing.md#sources-every-factual-claim-must-be-checkable).

## Platforms

| Platform               | Why                                                       | Tone                                 | How often                     |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| Nextdoor (primary)     | Geographically targeted: it's where neighbors already are | Casual, a friendly, helpful neighbor | 2-3 a week at first, then 1-2 |
| Facebook Page (second) | Wider reach; good for events and photos                   | A little more polished, still local  | 1-2 a week                    |

Use a Facebook Page, not a group, to start: pages suit one-way posts better.

## First four weeks

1. **Introduce:** say what this is and isn't (an unofficial, AI-assisted information site).
2. **Be useful:** share one thing a neighbor can use, with its source.
3. **Ask:** one question that starts a conversation, such as what to cover next.
4. **Share the writing:** link the first news piece and point to the site.

Write each post fresh from current, sourced facts. Don't reuse an old template message.

## Replying

- **Praise:** thank them by name, ask a follow-up.
- **A question:** answer if we know, with the source. If not, say we'll look into it and follow up
  within 24-48 hours.
- **Criticism:** acknowledge it, don't get defensive. If it's about our status, say plainly: this
  is an unofficial site, not an association, and the About page says so.
- **An offer to help:** thank them and point to the contact form.
- **Timing:** natural, not instant. A few hours in the day, the next day otherwise. Urgent or
  safety-related replies go to the owner at once.

## Rules

- Every post says Sprecher East is unofficial and not affiliated with the City of Madison.
- Never post as the City or claim any official status.
- The owner approves posts before they go out.
- No political content: facts, not opinions on policy.
- No personal contact details of residents in a post or a reply.

## What to measure

Neighbors reached, comments and reactions per post, and visits to the site from each platform.
Note them weekly; set targets once there is a baseline.
